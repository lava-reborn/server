# Passability of the global map, read off the map

Built by `data/build_terrain.py` from the 256 tiles of `res/globalmap/full` that cover the rectangle it reads, 8x8 pixels a cell.
The server's own table is lost; this is a reconstruction, and the checks below are how far it can be trusted.

> **The server reads the water of this table and nothing else.** LAVA had no terrain layer: a cell was walled because the location standing on it had no outzone that way, and every wasteland template has all four. The `#` below is what the photograph says, not what the world is -- it would wall off cells that open as ordinary wasteland, and it contradicts 18 of the 949 exits the settlement files declare. Where the rock actually stood: `data/borders.json`, `recon/analysis/borders.md`, `data/test_borders.py`. Everything below is about the picture.

## The checks

**Settlements.** All 227 cells of `data/settlements.json` are passable. 19 of them the photograph alone would have walled in (an icon sits on the cell, or the place is built against a scarp); they are open because a player has stood there, not because the picture says so.

**Map tooltips.** All 414 cells of `res/database/locations.xml` are passable, 10 of them forced the same way. Blocked after forcing: 0.

Of the 29 cells forced open, 23 read as rock and 6 as water. The wet ones are shore: 5863,1946, 5879,2011, 5970,1727, 6017,2086, 6032,1951, 6185,1977 -- a place standing on the bank of the lake covers a cell whose 8x8 block is mostly water.

**Connectivity.** From the start cell 6268,1819 (the black market of Wadi Halfa) 228892 cells are reachable on foot, 99.9% of the passable ground, and 224 of the 227 settlements. The world falls into 43 separate pieces of walkable ground; the main one holds everything but the pockets listed here.

## Where to distrust this

* Cells under an icon or a caption are not measured at all, only filled in from their neighbours: 9625 cells of the map are more than a quarter painted over.
* The 3 settlements listed above sit in pockets the walker cannot reach. Some of that is real -- the valley is meant to be sealed -- and some is the picture being harsher than the game was.
* Roughness is a proxy for relief. A field of dark boulders reads as a mountain; a smooth dune field reads as a road. The threshold (22) was chosen as the loosest one that still closes the valley.
* Water is only Lake Nasser and the Nile: 0.65% of the map. Narrow stretches of the river are drawn thinner than a cell, so the river does not cut the map in two the way the real one does.
